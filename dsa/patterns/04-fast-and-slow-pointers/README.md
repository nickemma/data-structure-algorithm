# Pattern 04 — Fast & Slow Pointers

**Status:** outline only. Follow [the curriculum](../../../CURRICULUM.md) for prerequisites and learning order.

## What it is (one line)

Two pointers moving at different speeds through a linked list, so you can find cycles and midpoints in one pass with no extra memory.

## When to reach for it

From the article: "detect cycle in linked list" or "find middle of linked list" in "one pass with O(1) space".

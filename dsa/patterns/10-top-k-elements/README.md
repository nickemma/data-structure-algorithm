# Pattern 10 — Top K Elements

**Status:** outline only. Follow [the curriculum](../../../CURRICULUM.md) for prerequisites and learning order.

## What it is (one line)

A heap of size k: you never sort the whole input, you just keep the k that matter.

## When to reach for it

From the article: "find the top k smallest or largest elements" or "find the k most frequent elements".

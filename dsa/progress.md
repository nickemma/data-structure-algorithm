## Spaced repetition

The whole point is being able to do it *cold*, weeks later. When you finish a problem, add a row and schedule the three revisits. Solve from a blank editor — no notes, no old code.

Use ✅ if you got it clean, ⚠️ if you got it but slowly or with a bug, ❌ if you blanked. A ❌ means redo the lesson, not just the problem.

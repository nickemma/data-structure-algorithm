# DSA roadmap

You do not need to know every data structure before you start. You need a repeatable way to go from a problem statement to a defensible solution.

## Phase 1 — Build recognition

Study Arrays & Hashing, Two Pointers, Sliding Window, Stack, and Binary Search. These teach the most reusable ways to scan a collection and reduce search space.

## Phase 2 — Model relationships

Study Linked Lists, Trees, Tries, Heaps, and Graphs. Draw the nodes and edges before writing code.

## Phase 3 — Explore possibilities

Study Backtracking, Intervals, and Dynamic Programming. Learn to state the choice, the state, and the base case out loud.

## Weekly rhythm

| Day | Work |
| --- | --- |
| Mon–Thu | One pattern lesson or one new problem (45–75 minutes) |
| Fri | Re-solve two previously learned problems without notes |
| Sat | One timed mock-interview packet |
| Sun | Review the summary sheet and update weak-pattern notes |

Quality beats volume: do not move on until you can explain the invariant, complexity, and a tricky test case.

# Try it yourself — Two Sum

Before reading anything else, spend 15 minutes on this.

For `numbers = [2, 7, 11, 15]` and `target = 9`, return `[0, 1]` in either order.

## Interview checklist

- What would a direct pair-by-pair search do?
- What information do you need while looking at `numbers[i]`?
- If the missing value has already appeared, where can you find its index?
- At what point should you store the current number: before or after checking?
- What happens if the target is twice the current number?

Write pseudocode and test it before opening hints.
